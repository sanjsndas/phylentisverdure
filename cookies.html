🍪 Cookies & Garden Data Stewardship

Just like compost enriches soil, cookies help us enrich your experience. We believe in total transparency — here’s how we use cookies to cultivate a better, personalized journey through our green knowledge archive.

🌱 Our approach to cookies

At Verdant Prism, we treat your data with the same care we give to heirloom seeds. Cookies are tiny text files stored on your device that help us understand how visitors explore our 78,000+ gardening articles, remember your preferences, and offer tailored content like zone-specific planting guides. By using our website, you agree to our use of cookies in accordance with this policy — but you're always in control.

🌿 Your consent matters: You can manage or withdraw your cookie preferences anytime via the "Manage Cookies" button below or through your browser settings. We never sell your data — our roots run deep on trust.

🌻 Third-party cookies & gardening partners

We occasionally use trusted third-party services (like seed catalogs, interactive zone maps, and embedded video workshops) that may place cookies. These partners comply with data protection regulations and help us offer free resources. For instance, our climate zone visualizer uses a cookie to remember your zip code across visits. You can block third-party cookies via browser settings without affecting core site usage.

⚙️ How long do cookies stay?

Session cookies are temporary and vanish when you close your browser — similar to ephemeral spring blooms. Persistent cookies remain for up to 12 months to provide continuity (like perennial plants returning each season). You can manually delete them any time.

🔐 Your control center — manage cookie preferences

We believe in root-level autonomy. You can adjust your cookie settings below or anytime via the floating icon on our pages. Changing preferences will not delete data already collected, but will stop future collection.
